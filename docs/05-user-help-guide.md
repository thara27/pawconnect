# User help guide

PawConnect helps you find trusted pet services near you, book them in a few taps, and connect with other dog lovers. This guide covers pet owners first, then service providers, then features open to everyone.

## 5.1 Getting started

1. Go to **Sign up**.
2. Choose **Pet owner** if you want to book services, or **Service provider** if you offer them.
3. Enter your name, email and a password of at least 8 characters, then select **Sign up**. Or select **Continue with Google**.
4. Open the verification email and select the link. If it did not arrive, use **Resend verification** on the login page.
5. If you signed up with Google, pick your role on the next screen.

**Forgot your password?** Select **Forgot password** on the login page, enter your email, and follow the link to set a new one.

## 5.2 For pet owners

**Set up your profile.** Open the profile menu (your avatar) and choose **Profile**. Add your display name, phone, city and a photo (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 2 MB). Your display name is what others see on community posts and reviews.

**Add your pets.** Go to **My Dogs**, then **Add pet**.

1. Enter the name (required) and species, plus breed, age, weight and gender.
2. Add health details: vaccinations (general, rabies, DHPP), deworming dates, neutered, blood type and microchip ID.
3. Upload a photo (up to 1 MB) and save.

Open any pet to view, edit or delete it. Only you can see your pets.

**Find a service.** Go to **Services**. Filter by city, service type (vet, groomer, dog walker, boarder, trainer, food supplier), available now, and minimum rating. Copy the page address to share your search. Select a provider to see their details, opening hours and reviews.

**Book a service.** On a provider's page, select **Book**:

1. Choose the pet.
2. Choose a date (today or later).
3. Choose a free 1-hour time slot. Greyed slots are taken.
4. Add any notes for the provider and confirm.

Your booking shows as **Pending** until the provider confirms it. You get a notification when it is confirmed, completed or cancelled.

**Manage bookings.** Go to **Bookings**. You can cancel a booking while it is pending or confirmed.

**Leave a review.** After the provider marks a booking **Completed**, a review form appears on that booking. Pick 1 to 5 stars and add a comment. You can review each provider once.

## 5.3 For service providers

**Create your business profile.** Go to **My Profile**, then **Edit**. Fill in:

- Business name, service type and description
- Address, city, state and 6-digit pincode
- Phone (Indian mobile number) and website
- Price from and to, and whether it is per visit, hour, day or month
- Years of experience, licence number and a logo or photo (up to 1 MB)
- **Available** toggle: turn it off to hide from "available now" searches

**Set your hours.** In the same form, set open and close times for each day you work. Pet owners can book any full hour inside those times.

**Handle bookings.** Go to **Bookings**. New requests arrive in **Pending**, live, with a pop-up message.

| Booking is | You can |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | **Confirm** or **Cancel** (you are asked for a reason) |
| Confirmed | **Mark completed** after the visit, or **Cancel** |
| Completed or Cancelled | View only |

Marking a booking completed lets the owner review you. Your dashboard shows booking counts and revenue from completed bookings.

## 5.4 For everyone

- **Notifications**: the bell shows unread updates. Open it to read them, or mark all as read.
- **Community**: read posts without an account. Sign in to create a post (question, tip, story, blood request, or lost and found) with up to 5 tags, or to reply.
- **Breeds**: browse or search the breed directory for temperament, energy, grooming, India climate suitability, care tips, health issues and feeding.
- **Contact us**: use the Contact page; we reply by email.
- **Newsletter**: enter your email on the home page or in the footer.
- **Settings**: shows your email and sign-in method, and lets you sign out.

## 5.5 FAQs and troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No verification email | Check spam, then use **Resend verification** on the login page. |
| Google sign-in asks for a role | This is a one-time step for new Google accounts; pick pet owner or service provider. |
| No time slots for a date | The provider is closed that day, or all slots are booked. Try another date. |
| "That slot is no longer available" | Someone booked it first. Pick another slot. |
| I can't leave a review | The provider must mark the booking completed first, and you can review each provider only once. |
| Photo upload fails | Use JPG, PNG or WebP; pet and provider photos up to 1 MB, profile photos up to 2 MB. |
| Phone or pincode rejected | Use a 10-digit Indian mobile number (optionally +91) and a 6-digit pincode. |
| Can I change my role? | Not from the app today; contact us. |
| Can I edit or delete a post, reply or review? | Not yet. Contact us if something needs removing. |
| Can I pay online? | Not yet. Arrange payment with the provider. |
